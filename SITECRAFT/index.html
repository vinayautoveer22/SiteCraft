What does each package include?

Each package includes Shopify store setup and a mobile responsive layout. Theme styling, product limits, payment and shipping setup, and SEO vary by package. Compare the packages.

Are Shopify subscription fees included?

No. Shopify bills its subscription directly to you; it is separate from SiteCraft's one-time development package.

Who provides product photos and details?

You provide your product details, images and brand content. SiteCraft handles the store setup, structure and presentation.
