Refund Policy

How refunds and cancellations are handled for SiteCraft projects.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Advance Payment

Projects begin once an advance payment is received. This advance secures your project slot and covers initial setup and planning work.

2. Cancellation Before Development Starts

If you cancel before any development work has started, a partial refund of the advance may be considered at SiteCraft's discretion, minus any work already completed (such as requirements planning).

3. Cancellation After Development Starts

Once development has begun, the advance payment is generally non-refundable, as it covers time and resources already committed to your project.

4. Final Payment

The final payment is due before handover of the completed store. Once final payment is made and the store is handed over, that payment is non-refundable.

5. Revisions Instead of Refunds

If you're not satisfied with an aspect of the build within the agreed scope, we prefer to resolve this through the revision process included in your package rather than a refund.

6. Third-Party Charges

Shopify subscription fees and payment gateway charges are billed directly by those providers and are not covered by SiteCraft's refund policy.

7. Exceptions

Refund requests outside the situations above will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Reach out and we'll discuss it directly.

8. Contact

For any refund-related query, message SiteCraft on WhatsApp at 9910276130.
