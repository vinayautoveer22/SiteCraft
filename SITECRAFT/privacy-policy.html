Privacy Policy

How SiteCraft handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how SiteCraft ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or contact us about our Shopify website development services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to share with us, typically through our enquiry forms or WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Your name and business name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Details about your project, requirements or the package you're interested in

We do not use tracking cookies to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you share is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • Provide the Shopify website development services you request
  • Communicate with you about your project via WhatsApp, phone or email

4. WhatsApp Communication

Our enquiry forms open a pre-filled message in WhatsApp. Submitting a form on this website does not send data to any server — it simply prepares a message for you to send to us directly on WhatsApp, entirely under your control.

5. Data Retention

We retain client communication only for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver the project and provide related support.

6. Third-Party Services

This website may use standard third-party services such as web fonts (Google Fonts) and, where applicable, Shopify itself once your store is set up. These providers have their own privacy policies governing data they process.

7. Your Rights

You may ask us to review, correct or delete information you've shared with us at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to SiteCraft via WhatsApp at 9910276130.
